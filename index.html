01 · Operational roleplay

Let's say you run Elite Global Staffing.

Forty AEs. Five verticals. Bullhorn as the system of record. The brand is strong. Pipeline is not the problem. The problem is what your reps do all week to build pipeline.

[ Operational diagnostic · floor reading ]
08:14 EST · Monday
  1. 01
    Your reps open the week with fragmented signals.
    ATS exports, news scans, LinkedIn checks, internal CRM notes. Nothing is cross-validated. Confidence is anecdotal.
  2. 02
    They spend 89% of the week researching and planning outreach.
    Reading job posts, scanning org charts, assembling context. The research is the job, even though the job description says "sell."
    89%research
  3. 03
    Less than 30% of the week is real revenue conversation.
    What you hired the rep to do is a fraction of what they do. The rest is unpaid analyst work — and it isn't optional.
    <30%selling
  4. 04
    Generic AI assistants compound the problem.
    One model, every task, blurred reasoning. Reps lose trust the moment a hallucinated signal lands in a CRM.
  5. 05
    Reps act on weak confidence. The market hears it.
    When the operator isn't sure, the outreach isn't sure. The reply rate proves it. The pipeline proves it.
03 · The architecture

GLVE is a Composable Agentic Architecture. Six specialized engines. Defined responsibilities. Verified handoffs.

Instead of one massive generalized model attempting every task, GLVE separates operational responsibilities into modular intelligence engines. Each has a distinct mandate, reasoning boundaries, and verification ownership. The system orchestrates them through structured, zero-knowledge handoffs.

01
Validation
Role Validation
Confirms hiring activity is real before outreach begins. Cross-validates ATS, ecosystems, hiring signals.
02
Multi-Vertical
Buying Signals
Detects accounts showing intent right now. Scores recency, density, and signal correlation.
03
Intelligence
Stakeholder Mapping
Identifies the right humans inside qualified accounts — visible decision-makers and quiet authority paths.
04
Synthesis
Outreach Architecture
Constructs the right message for the right person at the right moment. Context-anchored, not templated.
05
Orchestration
Timing · Orchestration
Sequences outreach within signal windows. Coordinates channels, cadence, response state.
06
Delivery
Audit & Delivery
Wraps everything in a strategic audit and delivers verified opportunity packages back to Bullhorn / CRM.

Calm for the operator. Complexity underneath.
